Let’s set up your family.

Group each child’s phones, tablets and consoles, so one pause or schedule reaches all of them. About five minutes.

  1. 1
    Add your childrenJust first names. Jack, Penn, Ford.
  2. 2
    Check each phone’s Wi-Fi settingSome phones use a changing address that can break the grouping.
  3. 3
    Pick their devices from your router’s listNewest first, so a phone you just woke up is easy to spot.
  4. 4
    Review and send to the routerYou confirm with your passkey. The router then confirms back.
○

Setup doesn’t block anything by itself, and your own protected devices are never listed. Couvre-feu doesn’t read messages, sites or apps.

Step 1 of 4

Who are we setting up?

Use the name you’d say out loud. You can rename later. Adults’ devices aren’t added here.

Need a shared device such as the living-room TV? Add it as a instead.

A child is saved here first. They join the router once they have a device, which comes next.

Step 2 of 4

One setting on each phone first.

Many phones hide behind a random Wi-Fi address that can change. When it changes, the router stops recognising the phone and its limits stop applying until you add it again.

○

Only for your home Wi-Fi.Switching it off makes the phone appear in the next list as a new entry. Pick that one and ignore the older entry.

Step 3 of 4

Which devices are whose?

This is what your router has seen on the home network. Skip anything that isn’t a child’s.

Step 4 of 4

Check, then send.

Sending to the router.

Your phone will ask for your passkey for each change.
