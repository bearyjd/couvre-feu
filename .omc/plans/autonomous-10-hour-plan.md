## Core design and acceptance requirements

1. **Safety before mutation:** protected identities persist for include/cron; reject protected-target overlap, ambiguous SSH identity, conflicting/shared person-MAC assignment and invalid safety inputs. Test adults remain unaffected, including a proposed policy that accidentally includes them. No automatic unknown-device quarantine.
2. **Input confinement:** bounded desired JSON, explicit person IDs/MACs/timestamps/windows, duplicate/conflicting entries and malformed/missing fields; no `eval`, shell interpolation or unrestricted original-command execution. Check existing ash/JSON dependencies individually; an unavailable target parser keeps compatibility pending, rather than silently trusting a different desktop parser.
3. **State and truth:** atomic durable updates, serialized mutations and recovery from interruption; desired/observed revision and last confirmation; `status --json` reports observed state and uncertainty. Failed apply cannot report success. Idempotent sync/include/cron and stale-revision rejection must not recreate expired overrides. Expiry re-evaluates the current schedule locally without Atlas.
4. **Firewall behavior:** early rules before established acceptance; kids-only scoping; no dnsmasq/WAN modifications or broad ACCEPT that bypasses unrelated GL security. Native zone rejects alone do not stop established traffic. Document the early schedule mirror as a PRP deviation and do not claim broken-helper native fallback guarantees stream cutoff. Unsupported DNS/IPv6/offload paths remain gated.
5. **Clock and schedules:** cross-midnight windows, current timezone/DST, invalid clock and jumps, expiry bounds and cron tolerance. Resume removes manual pause only; current schedule may still block. Bonus/native-schedule bypass conflict remains deferred; no bonus endpoint exposed.
6. **Recovery:** install/uninstall preserves exact originals and original permissions/absence, catches post-install drift and refuses destructive restoration; interrupted writes and repeated install/uninstall have safe outcomes. Never weaken exact restoration into best-effort cleanup.

Three evidence levels are separate:

- **Fixture/mock evidence:** reproducible local behavior for command grammar, malformed JSON, protected-client refusal, locks/state, installer recovery, stale revisions, timing and injected failures. Required regardless of real integration availability.
- **Actual isolated integration:** real compatible fw3/iptables/ipset/conntrack proves established traffic crossing a schedule start, pause/resume, local cron expiry, firewall reload reconstruction and protected adult unaffected. Record runtime/kernel limitations. If unavailable without privileged installs or network changes, leave this gate pending; mocks never replace it. Native-rule/bonus integration is required later when implemented.
- **Live router acceptance:** entirely outside this session's local execution approval. One separately approved test MAC and exact scripts must verify the original 5-second cutoff target, adult DNS/access, IPv6/offload, service-off expiry, reload/reboot and restricted key. Firmware-upgrade acceptance is another explicit approval. Never label local results “live verified.”
